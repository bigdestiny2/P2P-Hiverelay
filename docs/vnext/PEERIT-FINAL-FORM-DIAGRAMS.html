Diagram 01Big picture — the whole system

Peerit is not “a database in the cloud.” It is many personal signed streams that devices merge. Relays only provide always-on doors and opaque storage capacity.

Figure 1 — Clients hold meaning and keys. Relays hold uptime and opaque bytes. Signatures are the truth.

Remember The relay never becomes “the Peerit database.” If every relay disappeared, users with local data and keys still hold their history; availability would suffer until new relays seed again.

Diagram 07Lifecycle of one post (end-to-end story)

Figure 7 — Time line of a single post in final form.

  • Creator offline after Store? Readers still fetch cells from relays.
  • One relay drops the cell? Other replicas + client repair / re-put policy.
  • Relay tries to inject spam? Signature verification drops it.
  • UI shell updates? New public package on T1; social history stays in cells.

How to read this whole pack If someone asks “where is my post?” in the final world: sealed in cells on relays you chose, openable with your capabilities, verified with your author key, rendered by your client. If they ask “where is the website?”: public package on T1 seeders, opened through an HTTPS door.
