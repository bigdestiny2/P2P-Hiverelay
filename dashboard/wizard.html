Welcome to HiveRelay.

Earn sats for blind seeding P2P apps.

Your Umbrel is about to become a paid relay for the Pear/Holepunch app ecosystem. You'll host encrypted Hyperdrives — content the relay can't read but can verify and serve — so apps stay reachable while their users are offline.

Setup takes a couple of minutes. We'll name your relay, optionally add a payout destination, and pick a default policy for incoming requests. You can change everything later.

Honest expectations: HiveRelay is a new network. Early relays may earn very little while the demand-side market is being built. The pitch is "you already paid for the box; this is the switch to make it earn."
